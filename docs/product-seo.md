# Maa — marketing & SEO reference

Audience: **website copywriters, growth, and SEO**. Grounded in **`docs/product.md`** and the current product scope—avoid promising integrations or checkout flows not verified in code.

---

## Product summary (marketing-friendly)

**Maa** is a **cloud operations platform for courier and logistics companies**. It helps teams **book and track shipments**, **coordinate hubs and last-mile delivery**, **run operational and commercial analytics**, and **manage company setup**—branches, coverage, fleet, partners, users, and roles—from one secure, multi-tenant workspace.

**One-liner options:**

- *Run bookings, hubs, and delivery from one logistics operations workspace.*
- *Courier operations software for branches, shipments, and analytics—in one place.*
- *Multi-branch logistics: bookings, network, MIS, and accounts aligned for Indian operations.*

---

## Feature explanations (benefit-driven)

| Capability (actual) | Customer benefit |
|----------------------|------------------|
| Forward shipment booking & AWB-style orders | Fewer errors; single system of record for consignments |
| Bags, manifests, inward legs, delivery runs | Clear handoffs from first mile to POD |
| Branch hierarchy & branch-scoped users | Mirrors real org structure; right data for each site |
| Serviceable PIN / location master | Validates coverage before booking; fewer invalid routes |
| Operational dashboard & MIS / analytics | See today’s pipeline, delays, and delivery performance fast |
| Sales & purchase analytics (where enabled) | Commercial and procurement visibility alongside ops |
| Company profile, GST-oriented fields, invoicing terms | Consistent legal and billing identity on documents |
| Roles & granular permissions | Reduces risk: only authorized users change bookings or users |
| User invites & optional Clerk sign-in | Smooth onboarding; optional modern identity for enterprises |
| Subscription plans & usage reminders | Transparency on trial, limits, and renewal; offline payment flows |
| Procurement: RFQs, proposals, agreements + public quotes | Faster vendor/customer collaboration without extra portals |
| Accounts: AWB products, tax invoices, vouchers | Finance artifacts tied to the same tenant as operations |

## Target audience

**Primary**

- **Mid-size and growing courier / 3PL / logistics operators** in India (GST, PIN coverage, branch networks).
- **Operations heads and hub managers** who need daily visibility into bookings, OFD, and deliveries.
- **Founders and IT leads** who want one SaaS for ops + light finance/procurement instead of stitched spreadsheets.

**Secondary**

- **Finance controllers** needing invoices and vouchers in the same system as shipment activity.
- **Commercial / procurement teams** using RFQ-to-agreement workflows.

**Not positioned as (unless you add it)**

- Consumer parcel tracking as a standalone product (the app has **utility tracking/coverage routes**, but the core B2B story is **operator workspace**).
- Fully automated payment gateway checkout for subscriptions (current product emphasizes **offline payment intimation** and finance coordination).

## Suggested website content topics

These map to **real modules** and support SEO while helping buyers evaluate fit.

1. **“How to run multi-branch courier operations from one dashboard”** — branches, users, permissions, daily KPIs.  
2. **“Booking to delivery: what a shipment lifecycle looks like in Maa”** — forward booking, hub concepts, inward/delivery (high level).  
3. **“PIN coverage and booking validation for logistics networks”** — serviceable locations, fewer bad bookings.  
4. **“Analytics for operations teams: MIS, deliveries, and commercial insight”** — tie to dashboard + analytics areas.  
5. **“Roles and permissions: keeping bookings and billing safe”** — RBAC, invites.  
6. **“Accounts and GST-oriented documents for logistics companies”** — invoices, vouchers, products (no legal tax advice—position as software support).  
7. **“Procurement workflows: RFQs, proposals, agreements”** — B2B sales enablement.  
8. **“Security and tenancy: why multi-tenant isolation matters for 3PLs”** — company-scoped data, sign-in options.  
9. **“Subscription, usage, and working with finance offline”** — honest story aligned with product (trial, limits, payment intimation).  
10. **“Integrations roadmap: messaging, accounting, calendar”** — only claim what is deployed; otherwise “contact us for enterprise integrations.”

**FAQ seeds for structured data**

- What is Maa used for?  
- Does Maa support multiple branches?  
- Can we control who books shipments vs. who sees analytics?  
- How does billing work for the subscription? (Answer honestly: plans + offline coordination as implemented.)

## Tone guidelines

- **Confident, operational** — speak to dispatchers and ops managers, not only IT.  
- **India-aware** — GST, PIN, IST-friendly analytics copy where relevant.  
- **Transparent** — where the product uses manual steps (plan changes, payments), say so; it builds trust with enterprise buyers.
