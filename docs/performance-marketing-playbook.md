# Doorear performance marketing playbook (starter)

Budget reality: **$50/month**.
Approved channel split: **80% Google Search / 20% LinkedIn**.

## 1) Channel allocation (month 1)

- Google Search: **$40**
- LinkedIn: **$10**
- Remarketing: include only after meaningful traffic/conversions are visible.

## 2) Conversion events to optimize against

- `marketing_signup_click`
- `marketing_demo_click`
- `marketing_contact_submit`
- `marketing_thank_you_view`

## 3) Campaign structure

### Campaign A: High intent - core software terms

- Ad group 1: courier management software india
- Ad group 2: logistics management software
- Ad group 3: shipment booking software

### Campaign B: Geo intent - major cities

- Ad group 1: courier software delhi
- Ad group 2: courier software mumbai
- Ad group 3: courier software hyderabad
- Ad group 4: courier software bangalore
- Ad group 5: courier software chennai

Landing pages:

- `/locations/delhi`
- `/locations/mumbai`
- `/locations/hyderabad`
- `/locations/bangalore`
- `/locations/chennai`

### Campaign C: LinkedIn - decision maker awareness + retargeting

- Audience: Ops head, founder, logistics manager, branch manager, finance lead
- Geography: Delhi NCR, Mumbai, Hyderabad, Bangalore, Chennai
- Objective: lead gen form or website visits (start with website visits)
- Message angle: branch rollout clarity, MIS discipline, transparent pricing/allowances

## 4) Negative keywords (minimum starter set)

- jobs
- salary
- tracking number
- parcel status
- customer care number
- free app
- franchise cost
- internship

## 5) UTM standard

Use this format in all ads:

`?utm_source=google&utm_medium=cpc&utm_campaign=search_core&utm_term={keyword}&utm_content={creative}`

For city campaign:

`?utm_source=google&utm_medium=cpc&utm_campaign=search_city&utm_term={keyword}&utm_content={creative}`

For LinkedIn campaign:

`?utm_source=linkedin&utm_medium=paid_social&utm_campaign=linkedin_ops_persona&utm_content={creative}`

## 6) Weekly optimization checklist

- Pause keywords with 0 conversions and high CPC.
- Move budget to ad groups with demo/contact conversions.
- Add search terms as negatives each week.
- Refresh one ad copy variant per ad group every 14 days.
- Keep exact + phrase match prioritized at this budget.
- Keep LinkedIn spend controlled; rotate only 1-2 creatives per month.

## 7) Simple KPI targets (first 60 days)

- Primary KPI: `marketing_contact_submit`
- Secondary KPI: `marketing_thank_you_view`
- Target CTR: > 4%
- Target landing page conversion rate: > 3%
- Decision cadence: weekly
- LinkedIn quality check: review lead relevance manually before scaling spend.
